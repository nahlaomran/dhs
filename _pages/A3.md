## Assignment 3: Visualization and Communication

This assignment focused on how visual design shapes public understanding. I examined how charts, maps, and interactive formats can make complex information more accessible, but also how design choices can unintentionally guide interpretation.

I paid close attention to scale, color, labeling, and narrative structure. These decisions affect how viewers interpret patterns, and they also reveal the choices made by the person building the visualization.

### Main insight

A strong data visualization is not simply one that looks attractive. It is one that helps the audience understand the underlying argument without losing the complexity of the evidence.

### Reflection

This project strengthened my interest in the relationship between data and design. I realized that communication is part of the analysis itself; the way information is framed can change the conversation completely.
