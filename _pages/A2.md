## Assignment 2: Reading Patterns in Public Data

For this assignment, I explored how large-scale datasets can reveal patterns in daily life, social movement, and urban experience. I learned how to read trends carefully and interpret what those numbers suggest about the people behind them.

Rather than treating data as a direct mirror of reality, I began to think of it as a way of organizing observation. This process makes it easier to compare communities, identify gaps, and ask more informed questions about fairness and access.

### What I learned

- Data can show important differences between groups when it is analyzed thoughtfully.
- Visual presentation strongly influences how people understand a dataset.
- Context matters: a number without a story can easily be misunderstood.

### Reflection

This assignment helped me become more intentional in how I work with information. I am learning that research is not only about producing evidence, but also about communicating it in a way that is honest, clear, and meaningful to others.
