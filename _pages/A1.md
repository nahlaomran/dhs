## Assignment 1: Data and Human Experience

This first assignment introduced me to the idea that data is never neutral. Behind every chart, map, or statistic is a set of decisions about what gets counted, what gets excluded, and whose experience is represented.

I focused on understanding how information can reveal patterns in human behavior while also hiding complexity. The exercise helped me think critically about the relationship between numbers and lived experience, especially in public and civic contexts.

### Key takeaway

The most important lesson from this assignment was that good data work requires both technical skill and ethical awareness. A well-designed visualization can clarify a story, but it can also oversimplify it if the context is missing.

### Reflection

This assignment pushed me to see data as a tool for interpretation rather than a final answer. It reminded me that the real challenge is not only collecting information, but asking the right questions about how it shapes the world around us.
