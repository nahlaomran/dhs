## Assignment 4: Final Reflection

The final assignment brought together the major themes of the course: data literacy, human-centered design, and critical interpretation. Throughout the semester, I have become more aware of how information can both illuminate and obscure lived realities.

This project encouraged me to reflect on how I want to use data in the future. I see it as a way to tell stories, identify patterns, and advocate for more just and informed public conversations.

### Final takeaway

The most valuable lesson I have learned is that data is a human practice. It requires context, care, and responsibility. The goal is not only to measure the world, but to understand it more deeply and communicate it more honestly.

### Closing reflection

As I continue to build my portfolio, I want my work to reflect curiosity, clarity, and critical thinking. I hope to keep exploring how data can support more thoughtful and compassionate engagement with the world.
